## Yard work in Bordentown

Bordentown covers two places: Bordentown City, a small historic city on the Delaware River with older homes on narrow lots, and Bordentown Township around it, where most homes are in newer developments.

## What do Bordentown yards usually need?

In the city, older homes and close quarters mean tight yards, mature trees, and attics, basements, and garages that fill up over time, so [junk removal](/services/junk-removal) is a common call. Township yards are larger and see more [fall cleanup](/services/fall-cleanup) and hedge work.
