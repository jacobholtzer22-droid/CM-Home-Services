## Yard work in Princeton

Princeton is known for its tree cover. Neighborhoods close to the center of town have older homes under large shade trees, with mature hedges and plantings, while homes toward the edges of town sit on larger, often wooded lots.

## What do Princeton yards usually need?

Heavy tree cover means a heavy leaf load, and oaks drop late, so many Princeton yards need two rounds of [fall cleanup](/services/fall-cleanup). Mature hedges and shrub borders need [regular trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) to stay full instead of going bare at the bottom.
