## What do sprinkler systems need through the year?

In New Jersey, sprinkler systems are typically blown out before the first hard freeze so water left in the lines does not freeze, then restarted in spring.

## What are the common sprinkler problems?

Common repairs are broken heads, leaks, and zones that will not run. If you have noticed dry patches, soggy spots, or a zone that stays off, describe it in the form.
