## Why clear the leaves in fall?

Leaves left on turf through winter block light and hold moisture. That thins the grass and can invite fungus, so a lawn that went into winter under a mat of leaves often comes out of it patchy.

## What does a fall cleanup typically include?

A fall cleanup typically covers clearing leaves from the lawn and beds, cutting back spent perennials, pulling dead annuals, and picking up fallen branches and debris. Every yard is different, so we confirm exactly what yours needs at the free estimate.

## When is the right time to book?

In central New Jersey the leaves usually come down from late October into early December. Oaks drop late, so many yards are cleaned up in two rounds. Book as soon as the leaves start falling, before they mat down.
