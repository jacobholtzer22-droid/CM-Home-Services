## Why trim hedges regularly?

Regular trimming keeps a hedge dense and in shape. Left alone, a hedge tends to grow leggy and go bare at the bottom, which is hard to reverse.

## How often does a hedge need trimming?

Most hedges take one or two trims a season, depending on the plant and how fast it grows.

## When should different hedges be trimmed?

Spring-flowering shrubs are usually trimmed right after they bloom, so next season's flowers are not cut off. Evergreen hedges are typically trimmed in late spring and again in late summer.
